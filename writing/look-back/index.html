look back

2026-01-11

if someone asked me:“why do i make art?”

i’d say its because of you.

We all try to keep some people in our lives that we cannot, and we just learn to live with that missing piece for the remainder of our lives.

i never really got the hype around chainsaw man, so i consider look back to be fujimotos best work to date. ever since i watched the movie, i wanted to make something inspired by it as the art and visuals are so insanely pretty.

so i ended up putting together this short amv in about an hour, just trying my best to capture the essence of the film.
