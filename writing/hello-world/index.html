Wintercress

2025-07-05

She’s always been a proper girl. I used to think she was just slow to open up ; that given time, she’d eventually let me all the way in. Instead, I began to take solace in the little rituals we had such as her sharing some nice productive article to me, or her teaching my half assessed brain a simple math problem. I loved making playlists for her, probably because she was the only person i ever made playlists for and she actually listened to them. It was enough, or at least, I convinced myself that it was.

I had assumed, early on, that all of this was quite deliberate, like the kind of mystery girls put on to seem intriguing. I used to think that she held back out of some kind of awkwardness, like maybe she was shy or nervous, unsure of how much space she was allowed to take or because of how uncomfortable i was making her feel. But over time, i began to understand that she was, in fact, just a very deeply private and self-contained person. She didn't hunger for connection the way I did. She had her own world where I didn't play an important role or one that didn't really depend on me. 

This was freeing in some ways. She didn’t demand reassurance or chase after my affection. She didn’t really want me to prove myself to her or anything like that. I could have been emotionally absent and it wouldn’t have caused a rupture. She never really had fancy requests or hobbies like other girls seemed to. I think this was my favorite bit about her and why I grew to like her so much. She was always confused about why I pestered her so much to buy an iPhone, as I kept swiping through different colors and sending them to her, hoping that one of them might spark something inside her and finally convince her to get one. But she never really cared about any of that. She had no weakness for shiny things, no expensive taste in outings, and quite literally no urge to be swept away by any sort of candlelit drama. Even when it came to clothes, I don’t remember her ever being obsessed with a particular fashion or making a fuss over colors or patterns like most people do.

But i would be lying if i said that it didn’t bother me on how little she seemed to need me emotionally. Like how she could be just fine without talking to me for days and how her world would still be perfectly okay. I cannot be ungrateful here though, she was an amazing friend to me and I ruined it by having feelings for her. I do think she shared with me most of her vulnerabilities and gave enough importance for me to not feel neglected. In return, I was too clingy and that made her pretty uncomfortable. But some nights, talking to her, I wondered if I’d misunderstood what closeness really meant or if I’d put too much faith in the idea that we were closer than we actually were.

I mean, I have always thought that she was the most beautiful girl i have ever seen. She never really let me have her photos. She always said it was a terrible idea for me to keep new pictures of her, and I assumed it was just one of those things she said to brush me off and avoid the conversation. She sent me one eventually after I nagged her and threw a bit of a tantrum. When I asked if I could save it, she simply nodded, politely.

She had a small mole that rests just below her right eye. It’s not too raised and contrasts subtly against her warm toned skin. Somehow it's positioned in a way that it draws attention naturally to her eyes without stealing the thunder from any of her other features.

She looked so insanely gorgeous in that one photo she sent me a while ago. She’s wearing a strapless black dress that exposes her collarbones and shoulders. And then she completes the look with a black choker that wraps snugly around her neck, adding a touch of delicacy to her entire appearance.

Her lips are mostly hidden behind the phone she’s holding up. I do love her eyes in that photo. They’re large and expressive as ever, and are framed with a hint of subtle eyeliner. Her skin is so smooth and glowing as her long black hair cascades effortlessly over her shoulders. Her right cheek is faintly hidden by her hand in a playful, shy pose. 

She knows she looks pretty and she friggin owns it.
