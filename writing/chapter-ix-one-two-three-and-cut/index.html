He sat in front of the window, watching the first snowfall of the year, recalling a happy memory from the times he spent talking to her. The Blackwing 602 rested between his fingers as he tried to find the inspiration to write something. It surely had seen better days.

And as he reminisced the good moments and the bad ones, he couldn't quite understand when that feeling had ended.

The snowfall blurred the world outside as he finally mustered the courage to write four letters on the paper.
