chapter i - wintercress

2025-11-06

She’s always been a proper girl. He used to think she was just slow to open up; that given time, she’d eventually let him all the way in. Instead, he began to take solace in the little rituals they had, like her sharing some nice productive articles, or her teaching his half-assed brain a simple math problem. He loved making playlists for her, probably because she was the only person he ever made playlists for, and she actually listened to them. It was enough, or at least, he convinced himself that it was.

He had assumed, early on, that all of this was quite deliberate, like the kind of mystery girls put on to seem intriguing. He used to think that she held back out of some kind of awkwardness, like maybe she was shy or nervous, unsure of how much space she was allowed to take, or because of how uncomfortable he was making her feel. But over time, he began to understand that she was, in fact, just a very deeply private person. She didn’t hunger for connection the way he did. She had her own world where he didn’t play an important role, or one that didn’t really depend on him.

This was freeing in some ways. She didn’t demand reassurance or chase after his affection. She didn’t really want him to prove himself to her or anything like that. He could have been emotionally absent, and it wouldn’t have caused a rupture. She never really had fancy requests or hobbies like other girls seemed to. That was his favorite bit about her, and why he grew to like her so much. She was always confused about why he pestered her so much into buying an iPhone, as he kept swiping through different colors and sending them to her, hoping that one of them might spark something inside her and finally convince her to get one. But she never really cared about any of that. She had no weakness to shiny things, no expensive taste in outings, and quite literally no urge to be swept away by any sort of candlelit drama. Even when it came to clothes, she was never obsessed with any particular fashion or making a fuss over colors or patterns as most people do.

But it would be lying if he said that it didn’t bother him on how little she seemed to need him emotionally. How she could be perfectly fine without talking to him for days, how her world would still remain intact. He cannot be ungrateful here, though; she was an amazing friend to him and all of it was ruined by his deep-seated feelings for her. A longing he was never able to shake away, ultimately concealing the troubling recognition that this might not turn out the way he constantly dreamed of.

But then again, some dreams do come true, don't they?

She had shared most of her vulnerabilities with him and gave him enough importance to avoid feeling neglected. In return, he was too clingy, which made her feel pretty uncomfortable. But some nights, talking to her, he wondered if he’d misunderstood what closeness really meant, or if he’d put too much faith in the idea that they were closer than they actually were.

He has always thought she was the most beautiful girl he had ever seen. She never really let him have her photos. She always said it was a terrible idea for him to have new pictures of her, and he assumed it was just one of those things she said to brush him off and avoid the conversation. She sent him one eventually, after he nagged her and threw a bit of a tantrum. When he asked if he could save it, she simply nodded, politely.

She had a small mole that rests just below her right eye. It’s not too raised and contrasts subtly against her warm-toned skin. Somehow, it’s positioned in a way that it draws attention naturally to her eyes without stealing the thunder from any of her other features.

She looked so insanely gorgeous in that one photo she sent him. She was wearing a strapless black dress that exposed her collarbones and shoulders. And then she completed the look with a black choker that wrapped snugly around her neck, adding a touch of delicacy to her entire appearance.

Her lips were mostly hidden behind the phone she was holding up. Her eyes were beautiful in that photo. They were large and expressive as ever, framed with a hint of subtle eyeliner. And her skin was silky smooth while her long black hair was cascading effortlessly over her shoulders. Her right cheek faintly hidden by her hand in a playful, shy pose.

She knew she looked pretty, and she owned it.
