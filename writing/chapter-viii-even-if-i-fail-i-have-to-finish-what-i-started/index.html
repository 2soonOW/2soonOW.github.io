chapter viii - even if i fail, i have to finish what i started

2025-11-28

“Shit, you got some nexts?” he asked, rubbing the sweat off his temple with the back of his hand.

“Bhai, not the 15s, give me the 25s” he said again, catching the familiar bilingual lilt in the cashier's voice, the subtle hints of Hindi leaking through his annoyance.

He'd been clean for years but that counter was about to be reset. He flicked the lighter and brought the cigarette to life, the first rush of nicotine hitting him so hard it felt like something inside his skull finally unclenched, as if he had freed himself from the bounds of an uncomfortable straitjacket.

“Fuck. this is fucking bullshit,” he muttered under his breath.

His phone unlocked with a single thumb swipe. One scroll up, and he could see the last message from her.

“btw i have a boyfriend. Let’s not do thi–”

He stopped reading before he could finish that sentence. He knew everything past that point was only going to hurt. He was never going to settle for second place in her life. He was never going to settle for a friendship. This was the line he could never cross, he was far too much in love with her to accept that. He knew no therapy was going to save him, he knew this wasn’t one of those “time will heal” sort of wounds either.

This was iris, and iris was the peak of his feelings. 

His heart sank straight to the floor. His pitch rock bottom, His anger snuffed out of him entirely. It felt like his heart had slammed into the pointiest nail imaginable. He looked completely defeated. He had been heartbroken before, but this was different. This was the closest he had ever come to feeling truly lost, like a part of him had just been forcibly removed. The revelation of a boyfriend had meant he had no way to proceed further. There’s no argument to be made, no point to get across. If a person belongs to someone else, that is period, full stop. 

You see, they had taken breaks before. They’d stopped talking for weeks at a time so he could try to get over his feelings for her. But he always slipped back into that comfortable space, convincing himself she might somehow grow to like him romantically.

She always denied having a boyfriend. She never shared her romantic life with him. Why would she? She had been very clear about her feelings for him right from the beginning. She knew he was crazy for her and that would have made it worse for him. In her own twisted way, maybe she thought she was protecting him by keeping the truth to herself. And it was easier to believe that than to paint her as the villain of the story. Easier than accusing her of lying when honesty from the beginning could have spared him all of this. Or so he thought.

“She knows all these people. She talks to them, shares her voice with them, while i fucking get nothing,” 

The inner voice in him began to take over. All the anger he thought he had buried, creeping back at it again, slowly poisoning him. It was the same anger that cost him his football dream, the same anger that got him kicked out of the Overwatch WC tryouts, the same anger he never ever wanted her to see.

The insecurity in him deepened, settling into something more sinister and darker. And in that moment he got himself ready to dig for more about her in a vile act of desperation he already knew he was going to regret. He had crossed the line, multiple times by now. He was too deep into stalking her and finding truths about her, especially when she had made it clear that there was nothing possible between them. He had always liked observing her, paying attention to the smallest things, but this was far beyond that. This was an obsession, plain and simple, and he knew it. He felt it boiling under his skin, felt every inch of wrongfulness of it even as he kept going.

“Freak,” she used to call him, every time he uncovered something she thought was hidden. He brushed off the insults, at least on the surface, but what actually pierced him was the lies she had told him while saying it. The way her words treated him like he was some kind of stranger.

The way she had let go of him in an instant, the way it never hurt her to lose him, the way it hurt him to lose her.

He kept telling himself he was only searching for the truth, only trying to decipher the signals she had left him with. But that was a lie he fed himself to feel cleaner than he really was. Because the deeper he dug, the more he realized he had messed it all up. He thought that by stripping everything down to the core, by removing every doubt and every hidden piece, he would finally discover something honest underneath. But he never stopped to consider that the very essence of what made her who she was might get lost in that process.

It was like pulling at an errant thread in a sweater, thinking he could fix something small. But every tug brought up another thread, and then another. And before he even understood what he was doing, he was left with a heap of truths, all but useless. There was no comfort, no redemption, and there was no her. 

She was long gone by then. 

He had realized by now that stalking her would not bring her back. Nothing was ever going to bring her back. She was the biggest part of his life and he was only a part of her life. And at some point, he had to accept that some people simply belong to someone else, no matter how hard it is to swallow the truth.

“everything about our friendship is a fucking joke.”

His fingers hovered over the last message he had sent to her as he deleted his Discord for the last time.

He stood up slowly, flicked the finished cigarette to the ground, and crushed it under his shoe. And as he glanced up at the rearview mirror, he spat at the man looking back at him and continued to drive away.

And that is the story of how he lost iris, forever.
