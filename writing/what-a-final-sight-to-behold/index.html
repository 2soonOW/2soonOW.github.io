what a final sight to behold

2025-08-22

The northern highways of Ontario are a different kind of endless. There’s no sweeping cliff sides or any sort of gull winged horizons. It’s just mile after mile of towering pines and empty roads, stretching beyond what the eye could follow. Canada is beautiful that way. I had been driving since midnight and my Kia Forte is not short of adventures. The farther north I went, the lonelier the landscape became and all towns started shrinking to gas stations while the communities dissolved into tree lines.

If you asked me what I'm running from, I wouldn't know where to start. All I knew was that I couldn’t sit in front of my PC anymore without overthinking every single detail all over again and going on Battlenet only reminded me of how I cannot reach her no matter what I do.

What a strange kind of ache it is to be blocked on every platform and knowing that no matter what you write, no matter how carefully you craft your words, they will never reach her. There’s this tiny hinge where the mind loops endlessly, caught between denial and desperation, searching for a way in where there is none.

And as I reached lake Victoria near Stratford, I eased the car onto the shoulder. I sat there for a moment, the engine idling softly. I turned the key and the engine gave one last reluctant hum before falling silent. The only sound left was the soft clinking of my keychain swaying from the ignition. I reached out and pulled it free feeling unsure whether to start the car again or leave it off for good.

I stood there, gazing across the dark water. “What a final sight this would be to behold”, I thought to myself. What a place for a restless spirit to roam for an ephemeral eternity.
