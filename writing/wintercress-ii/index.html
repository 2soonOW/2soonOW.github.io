Wintercress ii

2025-07-10

I wonder if life experiences might give insight into how and why people act as they do. On the surface, it doesn't make a lot of sense to those who have never experienced the gut-wrenching reality of being unwanted, or thrown away. Because these things damage your internal logic in all areas of your life, and make it difficult to find reason within your motivations. You start replaying conversations in your head like they meant something permanent, like they were some undeniable proof of a bond strong enough to survive any distance. I’m not saying any of this as an excuse for my behavior, although it might explain where some of it comes from.

Am I suffering from schizophrenia? Am i actually crazy? I don’t think so. I’m not so far gone that I can’t tell right from wrong, or good from bad. I knew exactly what I was doing when I kept on adding her back from 12 different accounts hoping that she would add me back somehow and I could try to talk it out with her. Honestly, it feels more like this is all self-inflicted which stems out of my own stubbornness and my refusal to change how I feel about her, even when I know I should respect how she feels about the whole situation.

Being a girl is hard enough on its own, and having a friend who’s relentlessly persistent despite her clear disinterest must feel exhausting and overwhelming. She had every right to push me away and I deserve every bit of the distance she created.

For the longest time, I convinced myself she was still there somehow. I have been in denial for so long I can't actually separate reality from the world I have built inside my head. For some illogical reason, I was expecting her to come back and talk to me again and that somehow, despite all the damage, a part of her would still care enough to look back. But the truth is, people don’t wait around forever. They move on.

Dodging reality is what I do best. I can't escape the truth this time though. I cannot be in denial any longer. She’s gone for good. At the end of the day, beggars can’t be choosers. I knew deep down that there is no version of reality where she comes back, and there is no fairytale ending for me. I don’t blame her for blocking me on every platform there exists. I don’t blame her for anything. 

This is, unfortunately, how the story ends. But the suffering is eternal; the suffering never ends.
