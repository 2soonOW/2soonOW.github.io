black mirror tier list

2025-10-11

my silly little list

i) Hang the DJ - shows us that when love is genuine, you’d fight against the entire world to be with that person and you’d do it a thousand times over, in a thousand different realities. It’s also accompanied by what i think is the best soundtrack in the whole series( featuring Alex Somers and Sigur Rós), which makes it so much more emotional.

ii) White Christmas - IMO, this is the best Black Mirror episode ever made. Still, Hang the DJ holds a special place in my heart, so it edges slightly above. I first watched White Christmas years ago, but rewatching it now hits much harder. The episode captures that psychological isolation of a person "blocking" you out from their life so perfectly it almost hurts, physically. I also LOVE the clever use of red hues to symbolize death, and how seamlessly multiple technologies are intertwined in hauntingly imaginative ways. Top, top episode.

i will debate anyone who disagrees with my top two and YES i have a stick with me (I WILL beat you up). There is a reason why both of these are the highest rated episodes from the entire series.

honorary mention and shout-out to Playtest and Common people, both of which are easily S tier.
