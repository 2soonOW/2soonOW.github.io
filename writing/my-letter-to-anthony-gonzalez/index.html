my letter to anthony gonzalez

2025-07-10

Ah, so where do I start?

I've been listening to A Necessary Escape on repeat since it's been released and I thought i should try sharing my feelings about this album. For some reason, this album is exactly what i needed in this moment in time and i actually first listened to it on a late night drive back home after a long day of work and it made me feel a bit stoic yet at the same time my heart felt like it was at peace like it has never been in quite a long time. M83 always had that sort of power to make me feel that way and that is why i love every bit of music that anthony gonzalez releases and i will cherish it as long as i can.

Listen, i am by no means a music expert so my thoughts aren't musically meticulous but more of a "what this album makes me feel" sort of thing. This is one of their newest ambient albums and man it is NOT often that an album feels like it was written with you in mind and each track here has peeled back a part of me I didn't even realize was still hurting. This album is more pop forward than Hurry up im dreaming and it leans way too much into ambience and atmosphere leading to more of a soundscape experience than just a random selection of tracks. Ugh life lately has been quite busy, enough for me to pretend like it's not falling apart. I am going to be very honest and come straight to the point that Echoes is the track that broke me the most. I think it's the best song from the album and it's just so classic M83 coded that it's hard to not feel nostalgic when you're listening to it. I listened to it with tears in my eyes and I think that's a very powerful thing for a thing as simple as a song to do.

I think one of the biggest standpoint of A Necessary Escape is that it's so naturally cohesive not just from a musical standpoint but more as an emotional experience. Okay this might sound a bit too chunky but every track transitions into the next with an intuitive sense of flow, like for example how the transition from Atlas Imperial to Artificial infinity has no dynamic shift in tempo but it works because it has its own emotional momentum to it. I think overall this album is like a distant cousin to digital shades volume 1 but has more quiet and peace to it.

I might be a bit biased when it comes to M83 as i've been listening to their music for quite a long time and I have a very emotional connection to them. I like how this album contrasts with their last album Fantasy which indulged in nostalgia and synth-rock while a necessary escape strips that very aesthetic to its essence and takes you to this quiet peaceful journey. This album isn't just A necessary escape, it's MY necessary escape after how cruel and sad the past few months have been. I love you Anthony Gonzalez for making music that makes me feel a certain way that no other artist has managed to make me feel.
