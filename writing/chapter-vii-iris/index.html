chapter vii - iris

2025-11-25

Iris is quite the mecca of this story. But what exactly makes her so special? 

“On ne voit bien qu'avec le cœur. L'essentiel est invisible pour les yeux”

It’s one of the famous sayings in French which translates to “One sees clearly only with the heart. What is essential is invisible to the eye”.  And he had always looked at her with his heart, which means his perception of her stretched far beyond what anyone else could ever see.

iris was :

Kind – Figuratively speaking, kindness is not a rare or impressive trait on its own. Anyone can be kind, or at least pretend to be kind, as long as they are not actively malicious. She was not your flawless, saintlike definition of kindness. She had her strengths and her weaknesses, and i doubt she would sacrifice her last meal to save someone in an apocalypse (that much was obvious from her famous middle school essay).

But she did possess these exemplary qualities that mattered. She listened. Like really listened. She never argued without facts, and was a very passionate person. She preferred not to work for big corporate companies that actively harmed people or relied on cruel, exploitative ideas. She never spoke harshly to anyone, and she forgave far more easily than most. 

iris was :

Insane at Overwatch - She was quite good at Overwatch. Oh not just “good” because of bias, but good because she brought structure to the overall gameplay. She had very great mechanics, but her most distinct quality wasn’t even that, it was her owning all her weaknesses and actively acknowledging them, something that is far greater than being good mechanically (rumor has it that there exists a 3 page long draft of analysis of her gameplay sitting in his draft).

Back during the good ol’ football days, it was clear how valuable that trait was. Coaches used to hunt for players who knew their limits, players who could recognize their flaws and then build themselves around them. Those qualities existed in her. She was dynamic and she was the best of the best.

Having played with rank 1 friends, friends who dedicated their lives into playing this video game, and with friends who couldn’t hold down a mouse button if their life depended on it, she still remained the most talented person to play with (although the bigger argument here is that the overall quality of Overwatch’s pro scene is embarrassingly low, but that is a separate discussion altogether).

iris was :

Ambitious - Ambition by itself does not guarantee success. What really matters is the discipline that follows, and she had that at her core. She held her values very firmly and knew exactly how to set her priorities.

She was an exceptional coder. You should have seen her obsession with Leetcode when it came to interviews, god she was so good at it. And like many Gen Zs, her long term dream was to retire early while working for a company that actually contributed something meaningful to the world. She was anti evil corpo girl to her core, always an unwavering +1 to that philosophy.

One other thing that she always impressed with was her ability to instantly deprioritize a said hobby and switch her mind off instantly to redirect all her focus toward something productive. She was a well disciplined girl.

iris loved :

Music - Her ear for music was remarkable. Once, on her very first listen to Intro by M83, she recognized that it sampled Lower Your Eyelids to Die With the Sun which, for anyone else, has a negative percent chance of being caught on a first listen, and that moment has never stopped being impressive.

Everyone who knew that she played piano knew that she played it very well. They didn’t seem to realize, however, the work, time, and effort it took to become that good in the first place.

Her taste in music was quite impeccable. That is the hill he would always die on. She did, however, have a few undeniable stinkers in her repertoire thanks to the super American coded girly pop she liked. It wasn't nearly as bad as he made it sound. But beyond that one outlier, her taste was far more complex and distinctive than your average American girly pop coded girl.

She had introduced him to more classical music than he had ever listened to before, and their arguments with music could stretch beyond the depths of what these pages could hold. She was open minded whenever she listened or talked about it and she was always willing to explore something new, a trait he found almost superficially, attractive. She was also the reason he stepped outside his comfort playlists and began listening to different kinds of music in the first place.

iris was also :

Mentally ill-Yes, that is right. At her core, just like him, she was as mentally ill as it gets. You could debate on how different they were in terms of their terminally ill behaviour, but at the same time, they were indifferent in terms of how logically a sound person would behave.

Her Top 5 Spotify Wrapped songs were always just intros from different albums, which meant you could never actually tell what she listened to the most of the year. She was as night-owly as a night owl could get. She was awake every night and asleep at hours no normal human being should ever go to sleep at. 

She also mostly played only female characters in video games. Every single one of her mains, in every single game she played, was a girl. She had zero interest in sports, which contrasted with how much he loved football and how desperately he wanted to talk to her about it. And she typed at whopping speeds of 130+ WPM which was borderline insane(and she was very proud about it).

And iris was :

Imperfect - Like any other person, she wasn’t perfect. And that is not a bad thing at all. Her imperfections made her feel real. She was also quite stubborn if it came down to it. She wasn’t always honest, but the intent was never coated in malice. Instead it was more of a habit, or some self defense mechanism to protect parts of herself she wasn’t ready to share. There are things about her he hardly knew and his curiosity only widened the gap between them. He wanted to know more, she wanted to share less. A very bad combination of elements. And that combination did not stand the test of time.

And that, atleast, is a bit about this girl called iris.

Disclaimer : As I draw near the end toward the final two chapters, one of the biggest inspirations for finishing this book has been Minari, and the next two titles are directly inspired by the film and Emile Mosseri’s incredible soundtrack.

Iris is about a person ; a very special person. But at its core, it’s a deeply personal experience and a prolonged journey of trying to hold yourself together when everything around you falls apart.
