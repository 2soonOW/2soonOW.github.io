the "ella" FAQ

2026-03-18

1. who is ella?
:)

2. what was her favorite color?
it was indigo (i’m like 90 percent sure).

3. what was her favorite anime?
violet evergarden was probably her favorite series, and a silent voice was one of her favorite films.

4. did she play any musical instruments?
yes, piano.

5. where is she from?
she is from the united states, specifically connecticut. and no, unlike 90 percent of people there, she didn’t go to uconn.

6. what was her favorite song?
i think anyone who listens to a lot of music doesn’t really have just one or two favorites. there are usually a few that come close. here are some i think were among her all-time favorites. (outdated knowledge unlucky)
i) rach 2
ii) look down from the bridge
iii) myth
iv) most songs from folklore
v) she liked moonchild from m83 after i made her listen to it :) (definitely not amongst her favs but going to mention that because m83)
vi) insert mitski albums
okay i don't know, she had tons of favorites.

7. how did you meet her?
overwatch.

8. so you never met her in person?
no.

9. what was her favorite destination?
antarctica. any beautiful places with mountains and snow really.

10. what was her fav subject?
math. this girl was a sucker for calculus. i kinda wish she had a bone of creativity in her body but i distinctly remember she had no hands for arts.

11. what did she respect a lot?
one example i can think of is that she really respected sal khan and the team behind him, who have helped so many students, since she herself got a lot of help from watching his videos.

12. who was her favorite artist?
very hard to tell. i don’t think it was a specific artist, but there were a few who came close. an odd thing about her was that she mostly listened to female artists, and her most listened-to “genre” would probably be girly pop.

13 . one thing you’d change?
i would have maybe not gotten attached to her so much. be a bit careful. and not underestimate the power of my own stupidity.

14. why do you do all of this when she’s long gone?
makes my lonely life a bit less lonely.

15 . her fav youtube channel?
i actually don’t know, and it’s an interesting question. but do we have any FAV youtube channels or any singular favorite in pretty much anything as we grow older? she was really into complex, thought-provoking videos, so probably something along the lines of 3blue1brown, veritasium, or melodysheep. or maybe something even cooler that i just never found out about.

16. her fav non-anime movie?
very hard to tell. honestly, no idea. now that i think about it, i kinda wish we talked more about films and i could’ve shown off more of my cinephile knowledge and exquisite taste( yes i have a big ego when it comes to media).
ps: I RECOMMENDED HER SUCH AVERAGE FILMS AND I REGRET IT SO MUCH CUZ MY TASTE IS SO COMPLEX AND I COULDN’T EVEN SUGGEST HER ACTUAL GEMS.

17. what was her personality like?
she was thoughtful. she noticed small things and remembered them. and i liked how she thought before she spoke. something i really needed to learn from her.

18. what made her laugh?
listen, at my peak i was a super funny person, so i’m going to say me. but yeah, it was usually the dumbest jokes that made her laugh, so maybe that too.

19. what was something small about her that stuck with you?
i liked how she would completely lose herself in “nerd mode” when talking about something she loved. it was full of energy, and honestly, it was REALLY fucking cute.

20. what was her biggest strength?
her mind. and her typing speed. she could type three sentences while i was still on my first, which made arguing with her nearly impossible, like how could you ever argue with someone like that. but overall, she was just naturally good at understanding things. a very intelligent specimen. shes chinese after all..

21. what was something she disliked?
definitely sports. not a single athletic bone in her body.

22. what was her daily routine like?
being a frigging night owl was her routine.

23. what did talking to her feel like?
calm.

24. what is something you wish you told her?
that she mattered more than she probably realized. that i didn’t want her to leave. and that when she was around, it felt like i had the world in my hands.

25. what reminds you of her the most?
definitely music. my own shortcomings with religion. whenever i see someone playing overwatch and realize we were honestly better than most people out there. but yeah, a hell lot of things. pretty much everything. and all the time.

26. what was her voice like?
i never actually heard it. kind of sucks, doesn’t it?

27. what kind of energy did she have?
she could stay awake for 20 hours straight if she really locked in(this is WITHOUT COFFEE). make of that what you will.

28. what was something unique about her?
PASSION. there’s something so hot about someone who genuinely cares about what they love.

29. what did she care about the most?
making enough money to retire early. going to antarctica. probably dating a nerd. HER STOCKS. travelling the world and music, of course. to her credit, she was a nice person overall. sucks things ended the way it ended between us.

30. what kind of conversations did you have?
some of my favorite memories of her are just the conversations we had. they made me value her more than anything else.

31 .what was your favorite moment with her?
i have too many to pick just one.

32. what was something you never understood about her?
a lot of things. mainly, why did she just abandon me and felt nothing doing so.

33. what did she teach you without realizing it?
ironically, being around her made me calmer and less toxic. and RESUMES! i got so much better at building mine because of her. so much better that friends have asked me to make theirs.

34. what did you admire about her?
how naturally intelligent she was without ever making a big deal out of it.

35. what did losing her change in you?
everything.

36. what would you say to her if she read this?
nothing. or hello, maybe?

37. what kind of life do you think she’ll have?
probably something stable. she’ll figure it out. she isn’t me.

38. what is something you’ll always associate with her?
late-night gaming sessions, all kinds of music, montages, career conversations… and love. also the fact that no one will ever come close to how she made me feel.

39. was she pretty?
like you wouldn’t believe it, my friend.

40. was it love or something else?
it doesn’t really matter what you call it. i just know i liked her. a lot.

41. why does she still matter?
because some people don’t really leave, even when they’re gone. you still see their face everywhere. and sometimes they are always in your dreams.

42. you do realize that have to let her go at some point, right?
i know, it’s the end anyways.

i would also like to dedicate this one song to her. it's called Echoes by 2WEI. she always had a thing for crescendos, and the buildup to the crescendo at 3:26 never fails to give me goosebumps. 2WEI has a sound similar to M83, and another similar band that comes to mind is CHPTRS. they all share that same cinematic intensity.

the first time i listened to it, it felt almost ethereal. an out of body experience. there aren’t many songs that can make you feel that way, so when you find one, you hold onto it OR in my case, dedicate it to the one person you would do anything for. only certain people can make you feel something rare, just like her.

the lights fade, it feels like, this world might be crumbling.
