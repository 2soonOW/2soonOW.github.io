chapter v - interlude

2025-11-08

“Let’s play another,” he chimed.

“I have to go to bed, i have work tomorrow, and we’ve played enough,” she exclaimed.

“But you won’t show up for a few more days again… and i don’t know when i’ll get to play with you next.”

“No,” she sighed, “We’re done for the night.”

Then the pause.

“Just one more game.” He mumbled to himself.

He stared at the blinking notification for a few more seconds. Her avatar still sat in the recent players list. It read “43 weeks ago."

His cursor hovered over the play button. Then he clicked it. The lobby music sort of just dissipated. And then, the game began to load.

“Just one more game,” he kept on mumbling. 

“Just one more game..”
