chapter ii - what it's like being human

2025-11-18

“What’s your biggest fear?”
“Homelessness.”
“What’s yours?”
“I guess… ending up alone?”

Before any of this makes sense, we need to go back to their beginning, to the first time they ever met.

There’s something unsettling about noticing someone the way you notice them online. It was two days before Christmas of 21’. The original plan was very simple : log into Overwatch, lose a few games, curse at his teammates, go back to sleep. But what he had not accounted for, was stumbling into the same lobby as her. They met on a Thursday night, and by Saturday, he was staying up until 5 am just talking about stuff that wasn't related to Overwatch at all.

He didn’t tell his best friend any of this, of course. When his best friend asked why he looked like someone who suddenly rediscovered religion, he shrugged it off as best as he could.

“bol, bhenchod”, is she pretty?

“Yes, she’s so fucking gorgeous, but it’s not about how she looks. It’s her mannerisms, the way she is so bright and talented. It’s everything else.”

“Motherfucker, you like her already?” 

“It’s the eyes, mon ami. I’ve seen them, they’ve got so much fire in them. And she’s changing me, you know? Those parts of me i used to hate so damn much… they’re fading. One by one, they’re all just… going away.”

“But it’s nothing serious, right….right?”
His best friend, sitting in California, cracking open a can of soda loud enough for the mic to catch it, had no idea what his friend was getting himself into.

“Nope. It isn't anything serious! just an internet crush that should go away”

Which, of course, turned out to be a lie. He knew it in his chest that he was in trouble.

She was changing him, slowly, in multiple ways, in good ways. His life felt reshaped, as if he had found a purpose now. He found himself thinking of her more than he intended, and more than what she had intended, but no, not in a way that could ever harm her, but in a way that could unsettle the longest functioning neuron in his brain. He loved sharing all his secrets with her. She was probably the only person who knew everything about him and he felt safe knowing the fact that she did. In a way it made him feel special and wanted. He found her beautiful in every way possible, and how could he not? his life devoid of color, had finally found color inside it.

It wasn’t going to be a troublesome thing from the beginning if he acknowledged the fact that nothing about this was tangible. You see, there was no shared space, nor any accidental glances at work, or something uncanny that he had to worry about. This was strictly online, and thoughts of her were starting to settle in the corner of his brain. All he really had to do was shut them off ; be realistic. But she had a certain way of shaping herself in his life. Over time he realized they were far more alike than either of them would ever admit. Both were compulsive liars, in ways so subtle and habitual that the irony almost wrote itself. We like to tell ourselves that a lie isn’t harmful if it lacks malice, but we forget that deception, in any form, has no place to anchor a relationship. It never was, never is, and never could be the foundation of something meant to last. 

“i wish i had a real memory with her”

The lack of a “real memory” kept gnawing at him. Because what is love, what is longing, if it exists only in imagination and in the deepest corners of your mind? And yet, even without it, she changed him. Every choice felt different when she existed somewhere on the other side of the world. She had filled all of those gaps inside his brain. After she was gone, everything felt diminished again. His life regressed. Life felt devoid of color again. Purpose just greyed itself out. Joy became something he could no longer locate, no matter how hard he looked.

“What’s your biggest fear?”
“idk...Homelessness?” she replied in the most unbothered way, as if she didn't take the question serious enough.
“What’s yours?”
“I guess… ending up alone? he said.


It is not the fear of being alone that scared him the most any longer. It is something more dangerous: the recognition of the possibility that he can never truly forget her. He understood that the unknown can pull you in and then disappear without warning, and being human means dealing with whatever is left behind when it does.

And that is enough pain, for now.

Disclaimer : This chapter is inspired by Columbus (2017). I found the exploration of human connection very deeply profound throughout the entire film. The idea that certain people can reshape your life resonated with me on a very personal level and so, parts of that feeling influenced the writing of this particular chapter.
