The loop

2025-07-10

You tug your blanket a little tighter because you keep waking up every couple of hours. I think that’s the hardest part, because the progress resets every time you wake up. The first thing you do, almost instinctively, is check if you’re unblocked. You log into the Battlenet app religiously every time you wake up, not to play Overwatch, but to see if there’s a notification showing that she’s added you back. You close the app and try to sleep again but this time the pain in your gut has intensified and you start to imagine all the scenarios where things would have ended differently. You start imagining her happy, living her life as if you never existed, wanting nothing to do with you, while everything for her carries on as normal. You know you are heartbroken, yet there’s nothing you can do to try and twist the pain around.

Now you lay back with your phone in your head and all the other notifications from other apps feel distant. With your already slouched posture you try to convince yourself that this is the last time you will ever wait for her. You press the side button of your phone, locking the screen and as soon as dull light slowly dissipates, you pull the blanket up a little closer as if it were to shield you from your heartache.

You close your eyes now, knowing that in two hours, you're going to do this all over again, just like you have done since the past 7 months, trapped in what feels like an endless loop.
