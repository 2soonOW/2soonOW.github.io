merry christmas, please dont call

2025-12-21

Time is not moving for me. Everyones gone and its just me and my anger.

The toughest part is that we both know why im out here on my own. i was awful every time.

Oh ella the golden girl you are. you were magic. i will never get over you. i miss you, i hope i never see you again.

and tonight i will collapse in this snow and die forever. And you should know that i died slow.
