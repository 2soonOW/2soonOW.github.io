i swear she was right there

2025-07-10

Wasn’t that hard to not notice her. Math addict, Leetcode connoisseur, great at video games. I glanced away. Just another person online. Another fleeting moment. Surely?

I looked at her again and she was still right there. Just a chance encounter, right? Now she’s my best friend. I’m her good friend. We play Overwatch and spend all night talking to each other.

I looked away again. Didn’t think much of it at first. But then I started realizing how pretty she is. How effortlessly cool her music taste is. So I turned around, and I scanned in her direction a third time.

I swear she was right there.
