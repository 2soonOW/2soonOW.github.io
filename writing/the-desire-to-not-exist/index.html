the desire to not exist

2026-01-27

part a : providence : i ugly cry and cry when i think about her. i hate her for lying to me. i miss her.

part b : happiness : she was the special girl. we get no more than two or three chances in a lifetime for a chance at pure happiness and once we let them go, we regret it for the rest of our lives. 

she was one of those pivotal moments in my life and i will never feel that again with anyone else. her memories warm me from the inside. but they also tear me apart. 

part c : anomie : losing status in the next few months. no pr, no green card. the harsh truth of an immigrant life.

part d : anhedonia : the joy is all gone. from everything. from everyone. lost opportunities, feelings i will never feel for anyone else. theres no redemption for men like me. i hate myself.

part e : l'etoile : i look at the stars and i dream about her. elle etait si douce. but i just feel so lonely. 

part f : melancholy : i had thought writing iris would steer some veneer of normalcy back to my life but i am too far gone to be whole again

part g : the last dance : her memory of me probably bears little resemblance to mine. she hated me, erased every message she ever sent. our worlds are just battles of contrasting memories.

part h : dance under the moonlight : dance. dance. dance. and resist the restless desire to stop existing and ending my life. the world is so ugly right now anyway. dance. dance. and dance.
