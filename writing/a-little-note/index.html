a little note

2025-12-14

As 2025 comes to an end, i could possibly say that it has been one of the toughest years of my life. I’d be lying if i said im not scared of the future. So much of it is changing, all at once, and it feels overwhelming to take all of it in.

I really hate socializing or talking to people, and i've gotta try and change that. That’s been true for a long time now or should i say ever since i left high school. There’s this ever so persistent feeling that i don’t quite belong anywhere. It wasn’t always like this. People who knew me in high school or college wouldn’t believe you if you told them im now deeply antisocial, barely holding things together most of the time.

Writing iris has been one of the best things i have done this year, probably because it’s the only thing that’s kept me from completely falling apart. I am very proud of it. This year hasn’t been all lows, but the highs didn’t hit me like how i always thought the highs would hit. I do hope i can find that spark in myself again, the rush of dopamine in my veins, the ability to genuinely enjoy the things in front of me. The same kid who thought football was the end of the world, the same guy who spent a billion hours in Overwatch just trying to get better than everyone else, no matter what it took. Or the same guy who would spend countless hours making montages for someone. 

I do find joy in writing now, though, so not all hope is lost. I’ve watched so many Sundance Film Festival movies( BTW these are some movies i recommend everyone to watch because they carry so much meaning with deep and introspective plots) and some truly great indie films this year, and i'm grateful that they helped bring back pieces of my old spark in watching movies. I also think people have grown far too antagonistic toward reading. It feels like we’ve forgotten how grounding it can be. I wish more people would take a step back and read more often to escape or take a break from this never ending social media noise. There is just endless content to consume and little heart behind most of these, and that is not good for us.

So yes, writing gives me something to hold onto, and watching all of these movies still continue to move me. M83 also just released a new OST album. And i am so excited for the 2026 FIFA WC. It is that time of the year.

And i finally had the courage to publish my first book ever, so maybe it isn't that bad after all.

Just, maybe.
