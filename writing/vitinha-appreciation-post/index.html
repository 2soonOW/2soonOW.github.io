Vitinha appreciation

2025-10-21

I often tend to be super critical of the players and people I love the most, and i remember absolutely fuming at Marco Verratti’s slightly inconsistent performances 10 years ago, despite the fact that i couldn’t count on two hands the number of games where he actually played poorly. He was just that good.

Fast forward 10 years later, and PSG has found the absolute clone of that player in Vitinha. Not only does he play in a very similar way, but he also excels in the areas where Verratti was weak, while being slightly weaker in the areas where Marco was an absolute magician.

He's the perfect ball playing pivot and formations such as the classic 4-3-3 could help Vitinha play in a more advanced central midfield role where he could be the core of midfield three that drives the team forward.

People don’t understand the level of skill it takes to control the tempo of a 90 minute game, or the amount of concentration required to excel in that central position. The short passes to keep the momentum and the ball control to dribble out of tight pressure: it’s such a friggin’ delight to watch. I’m so glad Vitinha keeps expanding his repertoire every time I see him play. The classiest of players, and my favorite player in the world at the moment.
