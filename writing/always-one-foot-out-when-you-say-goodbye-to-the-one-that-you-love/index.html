always one foot out when you say goodbye to the one that you love

2026-03-04

i) that one minuscule fleeting moment between sleep and wakefulness is the only time i don't feel that violent stab of loneliness.

the split second before the weight of the real world comes crashing down. and the real world means a world without her. the truth that she is gone. THE GOLDEN GIRL IS GONE. and anything related to ella is always accompanied by intense pain.

but who likes painful truths anyway. so i really just want to stay in denial and my delusions a bit longer.

ii) and if i wasn’t so fucked geographically and spawned a few degrees further west, i wouldn't be having to spend half my life trying to belong somewhere. and now i dont belong anywhere. unfortunately though, my clock is ticking. i am almost out of time.

iii) my faith is absolutely lost in everything. even in my own religion. to be fair , i don't even remember the last time i was religious. i am just so exhausted pretending.

iv) i love my car so much. as stupid as it sounds. i have slept in my car in the middle of night after feeling overwhelmed. when i was crying. when ella broke my heart. when i was lonely. when i had no job, when i had literally nothing, when i had everything, i had my babygirl.

v) the incapacity to love anyone else. to even love myself.

vi) eventually, nothing is like ella. she put on a black dress. and the day i saw her in that, it was over.
nothing has changed me quite like her. but sadly it was never going to work out. i dreamt of the moon when i cant even look beyond the clouds. i cannot compete. too poor for her, too obsessive, too much in love, too old, too sad, too cruel, too weird. too lonely.

no regrets though. she is just one dream away anyway. my forever ghost.
