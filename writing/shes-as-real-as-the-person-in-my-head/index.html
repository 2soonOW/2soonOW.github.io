she's as real as the person in my head

2025-12-19

The experience of missing someone you’ve never met can illogical, to most people. But the actual emotion associated with missing someone comes from longing for their presence in your life, not necessarily from the familiarity of a voice or touch.

When we feel a connection with someone we’ve never met, it’s often because we recognize a shared essence in them. This essence can be anything. It could be felt through conversations, or it could simply be a feeling of resonance or familiarity. In this context, missing someone is not about the absence of physical closeness, but about the absence of that deeper connection.

That being said, i've gotten too used to it. And that’s why in a weird way, it feels almost comforting now. Yet even that comfort is paradoxical in a way that the comfort doesn’t soothe me; it just makes me numb, which is strange, because that goes against the whole idea of what comfort is supposed to be.
