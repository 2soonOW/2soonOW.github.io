better to be a corpse and dream than to live forever and not

2026-02-14

NAQUI AHMED
FEBRUARY 14TH, 2026
1:34 AM est

valentines day. not that it means anything to me, or ever has. it's always made me feel lonely anyway.

nights that feel like the space between one star and the next. i dreamt of her again. more like a fever dream. theres no way to distract myself from this. no therapy, no ciggarettes are getting me out of it. she's always everywhere. her ghost continues to haunt me.

i will never be ashamed of admitting that i've always liked her. i am never going to be. i cherished the good times with her. and i will suffer in the dark ones without her. maybe forever.

but what chance did i ever have? a person like me. she was just so breathtakingly beautiful. but i had to try anyway. the only absolute is change. no one will tell me this is it. no one is coming to save me.

not her. never her.

i hope she lets her memory of me turn into dust and fade away.
