you're so, so forlorn..

2025-10-11

and when the crescendo hits, i'll think of you once again

All my life, i've been a dreamer. I know so little of real life that i just can't help but re-live my best moments in my dreams, for such moments are something i have very rarely experienced.

I am going to dream about you the whole night, maybe the whole week, or maybe the entire year. I feel like i know you so well that i couldn't have known you better if we'd been friends for a thousand years.

Only three years, and you've made me happy for a lifetime. Yes, happy.

Who knows, perhaps you’ve reconciled the dreamer within me, resolved all my doubts. If i had never met you, i would have never known that the smallest moments could feel forever.
