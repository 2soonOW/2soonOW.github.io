a pinch of luck

2025-07-10

I haven’t seen the northern lights yet. It's just one of those things that’s been sitting on my bucket list for years. I’ve heard that the feeling of experiencing them is more like getting close to an alluring mystery and I don't think that feeling can be captured in a photo or video. I wanna visit them not because they look beautiful in photos, but because it feels like one of those things in life that you just gotta experience for yourself. 

To me, they seem infinite. Kinda like how time felt infinite when i used to sit there for hours staring at her username on Bnet convincing myself it would turn green, even when i knew it wasn’t going to. I picture myself standing in the cold, somewhere far up north, staring at those beautiful colors splashed across the sky with headphones on and listening to Claire de Lune and just wither away in awe.

Would it be silly to reminisce about all the happy memories i have of her while sitting in front of a grandeur spectacle? Maybe with a pinch of luck i will catch the same colors as she had seen when she was looking at them. Or maybe i won't think of her at all. Maybe the lights are just lights and not a memory trying to find its way back. Maybe that is my Kismet.
