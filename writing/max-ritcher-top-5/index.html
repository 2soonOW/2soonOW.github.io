Max richter top 5

2025-10-26

Max Richter is one of those rare composers admired both by classical music enthusiasts and people like me who know little about the genre. I also know there’s a lot of classical music enthusiasts who don’t like his work, but for a casual listener, i think he focuses on emotional clarity rather than abstract complexity or like technical showmanship and that is good enough.

  1. The Quality of Mercy
  2. On the nature of daylight
  3. Of the undiscovered country (from Hamnet)
  4. Chorale - Pt 4
  5. She remembers

ii) Nocturne
