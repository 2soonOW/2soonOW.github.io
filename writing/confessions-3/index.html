confessions

2026-03-15

unfiltered

people don't forget, nothing is forgiven. naqui, you are NOT forgiven. there is no way back now.

this is not me playing a victim card neither is a plea for pity, or peace or anything of the sort. i don't hate her neither do i love her anymore. she no longer exists for me as a person in the world, but rather as an idea of the perfect person who brings peace to my delusional head.

you can call me petty, or stupid, or desperate. i am all of that and a lot more. but i am also better than that, and so much more than that. i was simply human, and i made mistakes. I JUST WANTED TO DO GOOD. i have had such a shit history and i have done so many unethical things that i wanted to fix it all. being with her made me better than all of that and more. made me feel like a different person.
none of the things i did were meant to hurt her, and none ever caused pain to her heart. creeped her out? made it weird? yes. but aren’t we all taught to dream? were they such heinous crimes on my end that i deserve to rot in hell for eternity because of it? she had SOO many unflattering labels for me. CREEP. WEIRDO. FREAK. and probably a lot more.

she knew i never had any ill intentions toward her. but i get it.  what feels right to me was never right for her. what would have been good for me was never good for her.

i have nothing left to lose. i lost her already. i lost everyone already. i am the sorest loser in this shitty ass world. i have lost her more than a year ago now.

confession 1) i confess to stalking her. i absolutely don’t regret it a single bit. i wanted more of her. i wanted to know her the way all the other people in her life did. what the fuck did all of those people do to earn a full chunk while i had to adjust for crumbs? what the fuck did they do to earn that apart from just existing? you say its not my place to question that but why shouldnt i? SHE LIED TO ME. SO MANY FUCKING LIES.

everything i did, i did without a coat of malice in my actions. SHE KNOWS THAT AND THAT'S THE SADDEST PART. and everything i did, i did it for her. regardless of how much hurt i was causing, it was never supposed to end this way.

confession 2) i confess to randomly opening her twitch every day just to see if she was streaming, even though i knew she never streamed. i confess to this obsessive behavior because i wanted more of her. why shouldn’t i have wanted more of her?

confession 3) 1500 out of 1900 views on her valorant tracker were because of me, all within a span of three months between june 2024 and august 2024.

confession 4) i stalked her spotify everyday, which isnt even her real spotify. listened to every playlist she made despite it being for some other guy. all of the pain i had been facing, it just came raining down.

confession 5) 500 out of 550 views on her marvel rivals tracker were because of me. i confess that when marvel rivals came out, it was so brand new that it didn’t even have a tracker yet, and i still managed to find her playing with the same guy she was playing overwatch with while hiding from me. NO ONE except me could have done that. i was just sooo good at stalking. i don’t regret doing it either. she was gaslighting me, and if i hadn’t done it, i would have never found anything out.

confession 6) i knew about her different overwatch accounts, but i didn’t really care as long as she spent time with me. i liked being in denial, i thought i was numbing myself from the pain and instead of stopping myself, i continued like everything was okay. i brought this upon myself.

confession 7) i always put her before me. ALWAYS. every time i made a montage, a random quiz, a stupid playlist, any sort of art, in my head she was the one thing i ever wanted and i worshipped her. not literally or anything but figuratively yes. if i don’t simp for someone i truly love, then what even is the point? i wanted her to feel special. and why shouldn’t she have? every girl like her should be made to feel that way. and i know i made her feel special before it all went to shit. before i creeped her out. before i turned into whatever this is.

confession 8) no one, no one else in this world, NO ONE loves her more than i do. call it love or obsession but i know what i am, i know what i feel and i know how fucking real it is.

confession 9) i don’t stalk her anymore. there is no point in doing that any longer. i gave her all i could give and she didn't want me and that is the finality of it. i have nothing left to give her anymore.

she was never lonely, and i always was. she let me go and ABSOLUTELY deserved it.

confession 10) i dont defend my actions. i dont think what i did was justified in any ways but i would change nothing. if i could go back and do it all over again, i would. i would, without a shadow of doubt, do it all over again.

confession 11) my heart is so fucking worn. and nothing matters anymore. not the words i have left to say to her, not the good things that i did, not the terrible things. all of it is just irrelevant. nothing.matters.anymore.

confession 12) no one is going to believe this, BUT during the summer of '22, she came online around 3 am at the exact time when i was thinking about her. mind you, before this i hadn’t seen her for 2-3 months since she disappeared after Christmas. what made it crazier was that she came online at the exact time i was thinking of her, and looking at her profile. call it fate or an odd coincidence but regardless it was that day since we started getting close. the rest is history.

"everything i did, i did it for her." - or maybe i just did it for myself and projected it onto her to make my sad, lonely life feel less miserable.
