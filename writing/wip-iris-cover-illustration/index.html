WIP : iris cover illustration

2025-11-28

28th november, 7:56 am - after playing around with dozens of different concepts and ideas for hours to capture the closest feeling of what iris is really about, these are the two early sketches for the cover art that i finalized.

version a - more mature; reflecting the grown up version of her, a significant change from the previous art versions.

version b - closer to the original style that i always go for, maintaining that familiar aesthetic from the previous art versions.

Both figures are inspired from my earlier sketches. however, i have decided go with version A as i think it brings in more depth to the theme of this book and visually reflects a sense of growth.

my overall idea for the cover is built around a split composition which captures the psychological barrier that separates the two versions of her (more on that later).
